# animator studio 2.1.4

The Animator Studio on **ReSkate 20261114**: everything ReSkate's newest update brings, with the studio in it. Your tricks, packs and playlists stay as they are.

ReSkate now names its releases by date: 20261114 is the update after 2.1.4-5.

## how to get it

- **Still on Animator Studio 2.1.3?** SETTINGS > UPDATES > UPDATE NOW installs it.
- **Let the ReSkate launcher update to 20261114?** That put base ReSkate's DLL back, so the studio is gone. Download `ReSkate.dll` from this release and put it in your ReSkate folder in place of the one there, as when you first installed the studio. Turn the launcher's ReSkate updates off, or it swaps the DLL back on the next start.

## what comes with ReSkate 20261114

- **Global illumination no longer hangs the GPU**, and DLSS frame generation keeps working when the game switches it off for a frame.
- **The park editor** flies right after skating or turning the camera, and can lay its snap grid over the world (Show snap grid).
- **The launcher's Mod Manager** installs mods from a queue, several downloading at once, with an ITEMS tab to leave items out of a mod; merging many mods is much faster.

The rest is in the [ReSkate 20261114 release](https://github.com/Dingo-Shenanigans/ReSkate/releases/tag/20261114).
