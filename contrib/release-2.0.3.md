# animator studio 2.0.3

A fix on 2.0.2, built for **ReSkate 2.1.4-2**. SETTINGS > UPDATES > UPDATE NOW installs it. Your tricks, packs and playlists stay as they are.

## fixed

- **A trick done on a higher pop than it was made for put the feet through the board as it handed back.** When a trick has no version for the pop you did, it fills in from another pop and plays whole, as made, with its own board. At its catch it handed back to the game with the feet worked out on the game's board instead of the one on screen, so for a few frames they went through the deck or hung over it. They now stay on the board you see.
