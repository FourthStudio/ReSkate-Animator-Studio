# animator studio 2.0.1

A small update on 2.0, built for **ReSkate 2.1.4-2**. SETTINGS > UPDATES > UPDATE NOW installs it. Your tricks, packs and playlists stay as they are.

## playlists on buttons

- **Every playlist can have a button of its own:** one on RB, another on LB, and so on. Before, only one playlist could be on a button at a time.
- The LIBRARY's **PLAYLISTS ON BUTTONS** page lists your buttons and the playlist on each. Choose a playlist, then its button. A button already on another playlist moves over.
- A playlist's own head says which button it plays on (HOLD LB / L1 - CHANGE), and so does its tile.
- SETTINGS > TRICKS and the ReSkate menu's ANIMATOR STUDIO page have a row for each button, and one to add another.
- With two of the buttons held as a move begins, the one higher in the list plays.
- The button you had in 2.0 is kept.

## fixed

- **Custom grinds stopped playing after a map change.** The studio now looks for the game's grind again on every new map, instead of reading the old map's memory.
