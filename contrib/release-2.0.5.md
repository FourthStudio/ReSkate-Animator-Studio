# animator studio 2.0.5

A small update on 2.0.4, built for **ReSkate 2.1.4-2**. SETTINGS > UPDATES > UPDATE NOW installs it. Your tricks, packs and playlists stay as they are.

## new

- **Quick pops play your saved quick pop.** Save the game's quick pop (its ollie or nollie quick pop) and put it in a playlist: it now plays on every quick pop. The studio picks it by the name the game gives a quick pop, so a saved Ollie no longer takes its place. With no saved quick pop that fits, your saved Ollie plays on quick pops as before.
- TEST IN GAME of an ollie or a nollie plays that trick on quick pops too, while you test it.
