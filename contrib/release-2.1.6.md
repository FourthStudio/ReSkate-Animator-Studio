# animator studio 2.1.6

A preview of a flip trick on its other pops, built for **ReSkate 20261116**. SETTINGS > UPDATES > UPDATE NOW installs it. Your tricks, packs and playlists stay as they are.

## new

- **PREVIEW ON.** A row over the skater previews the open flip trick on the game's low, mid and high pop clips. It fits the trick's keys as the game fits them.
- **Chips** show where the game plays the trick differently. They are THIS CLIP NEVER PLAYS (with a choice of fixes), KEYS AT n%, LEG OVER LIMIT and FLIP xN.
- **THIS GRIND and THIS MANUAL** rows open the other parts of the move.

## fixed

- **In a preview,** LOOP no longer keeps the studio open, the studio keeps a pop or label change, and Esc does one thing.
