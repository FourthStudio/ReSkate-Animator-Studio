# animator studio 2.1.2

The Animator Studio on **ReSkate 2.1.4-4**: everything ReSkate's newest update brings, with the studio in it. Your tricks, packs and playlists stay as they are.

## how to get it

- **Still on Animator Studio 2.1.1?** SETTINGS > UPDATES > UPDATE NOW installs it.
- **Let the ReSkate launcher update to 2.1.4-4?** That put base ReSkate's DLL back, so the studio is gone. Download `ReSkate.dll` from this release and put it in your ReSkate folder in place of the one there, as when you first installed the studio. Turn the launcher's ReSkate updates off, or it swaps the DLL back on the next start.

## changed

- **The studio has its own page in the ReSkate menu: ANIMATOR.** ReSkate 2.1.4-4 redid its menu and took the ADDONS page out, where the studio's in-game settings were. They are now on the ANIMATOR page, next to SANDBOX. OPEN ANIMATION STUDIO is still on the SKATER page too, and F8 opens the studio as before.

Everything else in ReSkate 2.1.4-4 (the new pause menu pages, Mod Options, the HUB, and the rest) comes from the [ReSkate 2.1.4-4 release](https://github.com/Dingo-Shenanigans/ReSkate/releases/tag/v2.1.4-4).
