# animator studio 2.1

An update on 2.0.5, built for **ReSkate 2.1.4-2**. SETTINGS > UPDATES > UPDATE NOW installs it. Your tricks, packs and playlists stay as they are.

## new

- **BIG DROPS: keep your own catch and landing.** THIS TRICK has a new row, BIG DROPS. On GAME'S CATCH (the default) nothing changes: off a bigger drop or a higher pop than the trick's own, the game's catch and landing take over at its catch. On OWN CATCH + LANDING the trick holds its own catch through the extra air (set where with CATCH on the timeline) and lands as you made it. It is saved with the trick, so a pack's maker can set it for the pack. It needs a catch on the timeline.
- **REPORT A BUG asks which trick and what looked wrong.** A short form lists your last tricks, each with its pop and which of yours played it. Pick the one, tick what looked wrong (feet, board, arms, legs, body, a snap, it did not play, the wrong trick played) and add a line if you like. It all goes at the top of the report, so we can find the moment straight away.
- **F7 marks the moment.** Press F7 while skating right when something looks off. REPORT A BUG then picks the trick you did just before, and the recording shows the exact frame.
- **Updates show up while you play.** The studio looks for a new version every hour and when you open it, not only when the game starts.

## fixed

- **A pack's trick on another pop no longer changes shape in the air.** A pack trick filling in for a pop its pack has nothing for plays as its maker made it. It used to switch to that mid-air; now it does at the flick, on the ground.

Also in since 2.0.3: saved quick pops play on the game's quick pop (thanks Norms), down a drop a trick plays its own landing, and no more foot twitch at the pop.
