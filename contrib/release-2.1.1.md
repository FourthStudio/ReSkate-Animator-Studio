# animator studio 2.1.1

A fix on 2.1, built for **ReSkate 2.1.4-2**. SETTINGS > UPDATES > UPDATE NOW installs it. Your tricks, packs and playlists stay as they are.

## fixed

- **One foot no longer stomps into the board on custom grinds.** A grind loop's keys could leave a foot a couple of centimetres inside the deck for the whole grind. The studio now keeps every sole out of the deck while a trick or a loop plays on a rail or on the ground, never deeper than skate's own feet.
- **No more flickering foot as a trick lands.** Right before a landing the front foot could jump back and forth between two spots every frame. It now moves onto the board smoothly.
- **Feet on the nose and the tail count as on the board.** A foot standing on the kick was treated as lifted off the deck, so it was not kept on the board. It is now measured from the kick itself.

Also: tricks play the same on every skater height; the game sizes the skater after the studio's keys, as it does its own animations.
